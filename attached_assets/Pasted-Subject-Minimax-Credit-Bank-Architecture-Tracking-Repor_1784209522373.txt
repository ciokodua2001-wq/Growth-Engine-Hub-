Subject: Minimax Credit Bank Architecture, Tracking, Reporting & Admin UI Recommendations

I'm about to complete the purchase of Minimax credits and would like to revisit how we're handling Minimax within the Admin Credit Bank before we finalize the implementation.

After reviewing the Minimax pricing model ($5 = 5,000 credits, $25 = 25,000 credits, $100 = 100,000 credits), I believe we should move away from using USD as the primary balance metric and instead use credits as the source of truth.

BUSINESS CONTEXT

Minimax operates on a credit-based model. Credits are the actual asset being purchased and consumed. Using USD as the primary balance metric can become inaccurate when:

• Bonus credits are awarded
• Gifted credits are added
• Promotional credits are issued
• Discounted purchases occur
• Pricing changes in the future

Because of this, I believe credits should be the primary internal unit of accounting and tracking.

SYSTEM DESIGN RECOMMENDATION

The system should be structured into three layers:

====================================================
1. PROVIDER LAYER (ADMIN ONLY)
====================================================

This is the operational layer used to manage Minimax.

Track and store:

• Credits Purchased
• Credits Consumed
• Credits Remaining

Example:

Purchased: 100,000 Credits
Consumed: 25,000 Credits
Remaining: 75,000 Credits

Credits should be the source of truth for all Minimax balance calculations.

====================================================
2. FINANCIAL LAYER (ADMIN ONLY)
====================================================

Store purchase costs separately for accounting and profitability analysis.

Example:

100,000 Credits = $100

Track:

• Total USD Spent
• Purchase History
• Effective Cost Per Credit
• Estimated Cost Consumed
• Estimated Remaining Value

This allows us to understand:

• Infrastructure costs
• Vendor spend
• Customer profitability
• Revenue vs. usage
• Cost forecasting

USD should be treated as a financial reporting metric, not as the operational balance.

====================================================
3. CUSTOMER LAYER (USER FACING)
====================================================

Customers should NOT see:

• Credits
• Credit balances
• Provider costs
• USD spend

Instead, customers should continue seeing only usage-based metrics such as:

• Minutes Generated
• Minutes Consumed
• Monthly Minutes Used
• Remaining Minutes (if applicable)

The platform should continue converting Minimax credit consumption into customer-facing usage metrics.

Example:

Minimax Credits
        ↓
Credits Consumed
        ↓
Video Generated
        ↓
Minutes Calculated
        ↓
Minutes Displayed to Customer

This keeps the customer experience simple while allowing us to accurately manage provider costs behind the scenes.

====================================================
ADMIN CREDIT BANK UI RECOMMENDATION
====================================================

The current Minimax Top-Up modal uses:

Amount (USD)

I recommend replacing this with:

Credits Added

And optionally storing:

Purchase Cost (USD)

Example:

Credits Added:
25,000

Purchase Cost (USD):
$25

Notes:
Invoice #123

This allows us to accurately track balances even when credits come from promotions, referrals, gifts, or discounted purchases.

====================================================

Reporting should be treated as a core requirement and supported from day one.

The system should allow reports for:

• Daily
• Weekly
• Monthly
• Quarterly
• Annually
• Year-to-Date
• Custom Date Ranges

Date filtering should support:

Start Date
End Date

Examples:

• January 1 – January 31
• Q1 2026
• Last 90 Days
• Current Year
• Custom Range

====================================================
DATABASE / DATA MODEL REQUIREMENTS
====================================================

To support future reporting, auditing, and analytics, we should not simply update the current balance.

Every transaction should be stored historically with timestamps.

Credit Top-Up Record:

• Date
• Credits Added
• Purchase Cost (USD)
• Notes
• Admin User

Generation Event Record:

• Date
• Credits Consumed
• Minutes Generated
• Project
• User
• Video ID (if applicable)

This will allow accurate reporting across any time period without requiring a future redesign.

====================================================
FINAL RECOMMENDATION
====================================================

My recommendation is:

1. Use Credits as the primary balance and consumption unit for Minimax.
2. Store USD separately for accounting and financial reporting.
3. Continue showing only Minutes Consumed/Generated to customers.
4. Keep Credits and USD metrics admin-only.
5. Build reporting support from day one using timestamped historical records.
6. Support monthly, quarterly, annual, and custom date-range reporting.
7. Design the system so it remains accurate even if Minimax changes pricing or grants bonus/promotional credits in the future.

Please review and provide your recommended implementation approach before we finalize the Minimax Credit Bank architecture.

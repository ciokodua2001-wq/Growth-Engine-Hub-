Subject: Shotstack Credit Bank Architecture, Tracking, Reporting & Billing Model Recommendations

Before I purchase additional Shotstack credits, I'd like to revisit how we handle Shotstack within the Admin Credit Bank and ensure the architecture is designed for long-term scalability.

Unlike Minimax, Shotstack offers both Pay-As-You-Go and Subscription billing models, so I want to ensure our implementation can support either model without requiring future redesigns.

CURRENT OBSERVATIONS

Shotstack pricing currently offers:

Pay-As-You-Go:
• Credits purchased upfront
• Credits valid for 12 months
• No monthly commitment
• Higher per-minute rendering cost

Subscription:
• Lower per-minute rendering cost
• Monthly recurring billing
• Credit rollover benefits
• Additional platform features

While I may initially use Pay-As-You-Go, I want GrowthForge to be architected so that switching to a subscription plan later does not require database, reporting, or UI changes.

====================================================
CORE RECOMMENDATION
====================================================

Just like Minimax, I recommend that Shotstack be tracked using credits as the operational source of truth rather than USD.

The reason is that Shotstack consumption is fundamentally tied to render credits and rendered minutes, not directly to dollars.

USD should be treated as an accounting metric, while credits should be treated as the operational balance.

====================================================
SYSTEM DESIGN RECOMMENDATION
====================================================

The system should be structured into three layers:

====================================================
1. PROVIDER LAYER (ADMIN ONLY)
====================================================

Track and store:

• Credits Purchased
• Credits Consumed
• Credits Remaining
• Credits Expired (if applicable)

Example:

Purchased: 1,000 Credits
Consumed: 300 Credits
Remaining: 700 Credits

Credits should be the source of truth for all balance calculations.

====================================================
2. FINANCIAL LAYER (ADMIN ONLY)
====================================================

Track:

• Purchase Cost (USD)
• Subscription Costs (if applicable)
• Estimated Cost Consumed
• Estimated Remaining Value
• Monthly Spend
• Quarterly Spend
• Annual Spend

This supports:

• Cost tracking
• Vendor management
• Revenue analysis
• Profitability reporting
• Forecasting

USD should be used strictly for financial reporting and accounting.

====================================================
3. CUSTOMER LAYER (USER FACING)
====================================================

Customers should NOT see:

• Credits
• Render balances
• Provider costs
• Vendor usage

Customers should continue seeing only business-focused metrics such as:

• Videos Generated
• Minutes Rendered
• Minutes Consumed
• Monthly Usage

Example Flow:

Shotstack Credits
        ↓
Credits Consumed
        ↓
Video Rendered
        ↓
Minutes Produced
        ↓
Minutes Displayed To Customer

====================================================
BILLING MODEL SUPPORT
====================================================

Please design Shotstack so it supports both:

• Pay-As-You-Go
• Subscription

Example:

Billing Model:

○ Pay-As-You-Go
○ Subscription

The reporting engine, analytics, and Credit Bank should work identically regardless of which billing model is selected.

This will allow us to switch plans later without architectural changes.

====================================================
DATA MODEL REQUIREMENTS
====================================================

To support future reporting and auditing, do not simply update balances.

Store historical records for every transaction and usage event.

Credit Purchase Record

• Date
• Credits Added
• USD Cost
• Billing Model
• Notes
• Admin User

Subscription Record

• Date
• Plan
• Monthly Cost
• Included Credits
• Billing Cycle

Render Event Record

• Date
• Credits Consumed
• Minutes Rendered
• Project
• User
• Render ID

This will allow accurate reporting across any time period without needing future redesign.

====================================================
FINAL RECOMMENDATION
====================================================

1. Use Credits as the primary operational unit for Shotstack.
2. Store USD separately for accounting and reporting purposes.
3. Continue showing only Videos and Minutes to customers.
4. Keep Credits and Cost metrics admin-only.
5. Build support for both Pay-As-You-Go and Subscription models from day one.
6. Capture all activity as timestamped historical records.
7. Support monthly, quarterly, annual, and custom date-range reporting.
8. Ensure future migration between Pay-As-You-Go and Subscription requires no database or reporting redesign.

The goal is to future-proof the Shotstack integration while maintaining accurate operational tracking, financial visibility, and customer-friendly usage reporting.

## BUSINESS RULES

GrowthForge uses Kling v2.6 Native Audio Pro exclusively for video generation.

Verified provider economics:

- 200 Kling Credits = $29
- 1 Kling Credit = $0.145
- Kling v2.6 Native Audio Pro consumes approximately 1 Kling Credit per generated second
- Therefore:
  - 5-second video = 5 credits
  - 15-second video = 15 credits
  - 30-second video = 30 credits
  - 45-second video = 45 credits
  - 60-second video = 60 credits

GrowthForge will track video entitlements using SECONDS rather than video counts.

# CREDIT CONSUMPTION ORDER

When a video is generated:

1. Deduct from Monthly Included Seconds first
2. Deduct from Purchased Seconds second

Example:

Monthly Remaining = 10
Purchased Remaining = 50

User generates 15-second video

Result:

10 seconds deducted from monthly allocation
5 seconds deducted from purchased balance

# VIDEO GENERATION PROCESS

Before generation:

1. Determine requested duration.
2. Calculate required seconds.
3. Verify available balance.
4. If balance is insufficient:
   - Block generation
   - Prompt user to purchase additional credits

After successful generation:

1. Deduct actual generated seconds.
2. Update user balances.
3. Update admin metrics.
4. Create permanent usage log.

Failed generations:

- Log failure
- Do NOT deduct any credits

---

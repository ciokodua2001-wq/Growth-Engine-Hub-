Owner's Corner (Private Super Admin Workspace)

I need a new "Owner's Corner" area built into the backend admin panel. This section must be completely hidden from all users and administrators and accessible only by me (the platform owner).

Access & Authentication
The Owner's Corner should be tied to a separate email address from the one currently used for my Admin account.
Only this designated owner account should have access.
No other admin, moderator, team member, or subscriber should be able to see or access this section.
Consider implementing a distinct role such as OWNER or SUPER_OWNER with permissions above all existing admin roles.
Unlimited Platform Access

Within the Owner's Corner, I should have unrestricted access to all platform capabilities, including:

Unlimited content creation
Unlimited campaign creation
Unlimited email sends (subject to provider limitations)
Unlimited storage/usage quotas where applicable
Full access to analytics, reporting, and growth tools
The ability to test premium features without subscription restrictions
Visibility into all platform usage and engagement metrics
Marketing & Campaign Management

I want a dedicated marketing dashboard that allows me to manage platform growth initiatives.

External Email Marketing

Create and send email campaigns to imported contacts.
Track key metrics:
Delivery rate

Inactive users

Since email marketing is involved, ensure the system includes:

Unsubscribe functionality

Compliance with applicable anti-spam regulations (CAN-SPAM, GDPR, and similar requirements where relevant)
Technical Notes
Build this as a completely separate permission layer from standard Admin permissions.
The Owner's Corner should not appear in navigation menus for regular admins.
All actions taken from this area should be logged for auditing purposes.
The architecture should be scalable for future growth, including advanced automation, customer journeys, and marketing workflows.

Goal: Provide the platform owner with a private growth and operations command center that combines super-admin controls, advanced marketing capabilities, user segmentation, subscription recovery campaigns, and platform-wide communications in a single secure workspace.

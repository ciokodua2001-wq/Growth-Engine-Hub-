First, I want to say that after reviewing the latest rendered commercial, I believe we are moving in the right direction.

After thinking through the overall product vision for GrowthForge, I've decided that I want to simplify the entire Commercial Studio rather than continue pursuing talking-head avatars.

The cinematic commercial generated through Kling is actually much closer to the premium experience I want GrowthForge to deliver. The visual quality is excellent, and I believe this approach is more scalable, more reliable, and much easier to maintain than trying to build an avatar-based system.

So let's officially commit to this direction.

Our objective is now:

GrowthForge Commercial Studio automatically produces high-quality cinematic marketing commercials using Kling AI.

I no longer want to build the product around talking-head avatars or avatar libraries.

Instead, let's focus on perfecting the cinematic commercial workflow.

That said, I noticed a few issues that we now need to solve before this feature is production-ready.

Issue 1 – No Audio

The rendered commercial has no narration or background music.

We need to integrate professional audio.

Requirements:

ElevenLabs should generate professional English narration from the approved script.
Background music should be added automatically.
FFmpeg should synchronize narration, music, and the video.
The finished commercial should feel complete and ready to publish.
Issue 2 – No Captions

The generated commercial contains no captions.

Requirements:

Automatically generate English captions from the narration.
Burn captions directly into the commercial.
Keep captions professionally styled and readable.
Position captions for social media viewing without covering important visuals.
Issue 3 – Incorrect Duration

The requested commercial length was 15 seconds.

The rendered output is approximately 27 seconds.

Please investigate why the requested duration is not being respected.

I want the production pipeline to honor the selected commercial length.

For example:

15-second commercial
30-second commercial
45-second commercial
60-second commercial

The final output should stay as close as possible to the requested duration.

Issue 4 – Foreign Language Text

Some scenes display random foreign-language text or characters.

GrowthForge is an English-language marketing platform.

Requirements:

All visible text generated inside scenes must be English only.
If Kling generates unwanted embedded text, investigate prompt engineering or post-processing techniques to minimize or eliminate it.
If the model cannot reliably generate English signage or labels, avoid requesting visible text inside the generated imagery and instead overlay our own English text using FFmpeg.
Overall Direction

Please keep the architecture simple.

Do not reintroduce avatar systems or talking-head workflows.

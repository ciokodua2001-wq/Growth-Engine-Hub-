Production Brief

Visual Style

Clean, high-contrast SaaS product demo aesthetic with cinematic close-ups — think Apple meets Y Combinator pitch deck

Environment

Minimal modern home office — white walls, one large monitor, a single potted plant, warm afternoon light through a frosted window

Lighting

Soft directional key light from screen left simulating window light, subtle fill bounce from right, screen glow providing cool accent on face

Camera Language

Tight close-ups on face and hands, quick smash cut to screen UI, final slow push into glowing dashboard

Character

Late-20s female founder, sharp jawline, natural makeup, wearing a fitted black crewneck, dark hair pulled back, radiating calm confidence

Performance Direction

Understated and direct — she speaks like she's letting a friend in on a secret, no performance, just conviction

Transform GrowthForge into a premium SaaS website that looks comparable to Stripe, Linear, Vercel, Notion, Framer, and HubSpot.

Current website is functional but feels too narrow and lacks enough visual proof of value.

The website should feel like a category-defining AI Growth Operating System.

DESIGN GOALS

1. Use a much wider layout.

- Reduce unnecessary side margins.
- Create a modern full-width SaaS experience.

- Hero
- Dashboard previews
- Features
- Product sections
- How It Works
- Testimonials
- Pricing

1-column layouts.

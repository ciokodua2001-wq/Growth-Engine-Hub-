Build a production-grade SaaS platform called:

GROWTHFORGE AI

TAGLINE

"Paste Your URL. Get Your Marketing Department."

OVERVIEW

GrowthForge AI is an AI-powered Growth Operating System.

A user pastes their website URL.

The platform automatically:

- Understands the business
- Identifies competitors
- Analyzes market positioning
- Generates marketing strategy
- Creates content
- Creates emails
- Creates social posts
- Creates advertising campaigns
- Creates promotional videos
- Publishes content
- Launches ads
- Optimizes campaigns
- Tracks growth metrics

The goal is to replace multiple marketing roles with one AI-driven platform.
